# Privacy Policy for Neon Peaks Solitaire

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy explains how Neon Peaks Solitaire ("the app"), published by Absolutezero91
("we"), handles information. Template only: review it and adjust to match your actual setup. This is
not legal advice.

## Information the app stores on your device
Your game progress (World Tour stars, coins, boosters, cosmetics and outfits), statistics, and
settings are stored on your device. If your phone's Android backup is on, Android may include this
data in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. We do not receive it.

## Google Play Games (optional)
If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app uses it for achievements, leaderboards and cloud
save. Your Play Games display name and scores may be visible to other players on leaderboards, as
set in your Play Games profile. Cloud save stores a copy of your game progress in your Google
account. This is handled by Google under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Crash reports
The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to receive reports when the app crashes. A crash report
contains technical information such as the device model, Android version, app version, the state of
the app at the time of the crash, and a random installation identifier. It does not contain your
name or contact details. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Notifications (optional)
If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the app schedules one local notification per day on
your device. No data is sent anywhere for this.

## Advertising
The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use information such
as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads, to
serve and measure ads and, where you have consented, to personalize them. See Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the app asks
for your consent choices before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choices at any time
in Settings under "Privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your
device settings.

## In-app purchases
Purchases (Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not
receive your payment details. Google Play shares a purchase record with the app so it can deliver
what you bought.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from children.
